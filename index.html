Questions

Before you run.

Is it free?

Yes. Olympus Run is free to download and play. Coins are earned by running; you can optionally buy coin packs, bundles, the season's premium track, or Olympus VIP.

What does VIP do?

No ads, 50% off every coin price in the shop, double run coins, 150 daily coins, an extra revive every run and double Olympus Trials XP. Monthly, yearly, or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

Are there ads?

Optional bonus videos — a revive, double coins after a run, the daily chest — that you choose to watch. VIP removes ads entirely.

Do I need an account?

No. Sign in to Game Center if you want leaderboards and friend challenges; otherwise just play.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later. Age rating 9+.
