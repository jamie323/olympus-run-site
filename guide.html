How to run with the gods.

Everything the game teaches you in the first hour, and a few things it doesn't.

Controls

Swipe left / rightChange lane. You can swipe mid-air.
Swipe upJump. Some heroes can tap jump again mid-air for a double jump.
Swipe downSlide under barriers and boss claws — and drop faster out of a jump.
TapStrike. Breaks gates and hits bosses when they're open.
Ability buttonBottom-left. Your hero's signature power, on a cooldown.
HeartsYour HP for the run. Hit something and you lose one; at zero you fall — unless you revive.
You can revive with coins (the price rises each time in a run), with a bonus video, or with a diamond life — collect 10 diamonds in a run to earn one.

Boss fights

A boss has 3 HP. It blocks the whole path and attacks lane by lane — slide under the low sweeps, jump the shockwaves, and strike when it's exposed. Defeat it for keys, coins and a big score bonus. In Random mode, every defeated boss sends you to a new world.

Pre-run boosts

Equip Head Start, Coin Boost or an Extra Life from your Loadout before a run. Earn them from quests, the season pass and the daily chest.

Progression

CoinsEarned every run. Spend on heroes, equipment upgrades, cosmetics and power-ups.
KeysEarn-only — from bosses, the season pass and the login calendar. Cash them in at the Vault Crates for a fixed coin payout.
Daily & weekly questsFresh objectives, real rewards. Every third weekly set earns a 30%-off voucher on your next unlock.
Daily TributeA 7-day login calendar with a streak. Day 7 pays 5 keys and a Coin Boost.
Daily chestOne free chest a day — coins or a power-up.
Olympus TrialsThe season pass: 50 tiers, 800 XP per tier, a new season every 30 days. Free track for everyone; a premium track you can unlock for the season.
MasteryPer-hero levels with rewards at 5, 10 and 20.
LeaderboardsGame Center, plus the daily "everyone vs everyone" rank on your best runs.

Shop & VIP

Everything in the shop can be earned by playing. If you'd rather skip ahead: coin packs, fixed-content bundles, a one-time Hero Starter Pack, and the season's premium track.

Olympus VIP (monthly, yearly, or Lifetime): no ads, 50% off every coin price, 2× run coins, 150 daily coins, an extra revive every run and 2× Trials XP. Subscriptions renew until cancelled; manage them in your Apple ID settings.

Tips from the mountain

Slide is faster than jump. When in doubt, go under.
Save your signature power for the boss — Shield Bash or Divine Shield turns a claw sweep into free hits.
Vertigo is worth it. 3× coins for eight seconds is the biggest payday in the game; switch lanes with the screen, not against it.
Never let the feather pass. Sky Flight is both safety and money.
The Mage's Time Warp is a boss-fight cheat code: 4 seconds at 40% speed to line up strikes.
Coin revive prices climb each time you use one in a run — take the free video revive first.
Play the Daily Tribute first every day; the streak milestones are where the keys are.
A new high score shows how many players beat you today. That number is the real game.
